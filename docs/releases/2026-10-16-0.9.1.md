# WanderPlan 0.9.1 — Integrated QA fixes (R16/R08)

Result of the integrated QA, mobile and security pass on 0.9.0.

## Fixed

- **D1 — members with money involvement (customer rule A023).** The owner can no longer remove a member who still owes money, is still owed money (actual expenses minus recorded payments), or is on an expense that has no actual amount yet; the message says how much or what to settle. After a settled member is removed, old expenses show them as "(đã rời nhóm)" in the list, settlement and form, stay editable, and keep who paid and who took part; former members cannot be added to other expenses. Excel re-imports keep them on the rows they belong to. Previously such expenses could no longer be edited.
- **D2 — touch targets.** Notice "Đóng" buttons are now at least 44 × 44 px.
- **Hardening.** Trigger functions are no longer executable through the API (they still run as triggers; new-account profile creation verified). Direct participant updates are limited to split values.

## QA summary (0.9.0 baseline)

- Automated: 106 application tests (22 suites), lint, build, npm audit 0; 258 SQL assertions (14 suites) on embedded PostgreSQL and full local Supabase; schema lint clean.
- Two-account UAT on the local stack: trip create/select, invitation seat reservation, decline and re-invite, accept, display names, checklist with assignee and Realtime, itinerary with Maps https rule, round-trip and stay ranges, settlement confirmation, archive/restore (member hidden instantly, data and payments restored), member removal and the D1 fix end to end (blocked for estimate, blocked for 50,000 đ debt, allowed after confirmation, old expense edited with former payer kept).
- Responsive: Login, Tổng quan, Lịch trình, Chi phí, Nhóm on a long-name trip at 320/375/768/1280 and expense/trip dialogs at 320 — no horizontal overflow.
- Security: RLS on every public table, no SECURITY DEFINER without search_path, removed members read nothing from the trip, anonymous access limited to platform-default grants that PostgREST does not expose. Legacy `claim_trip_invitations` (no-op) and `create_default_trip` stay callable for compatibility; accepted as low risk.

## Rollout and recovery

Apply `20261016230000_settled_member_removal.sql` before the frontend; it changes no rows. Prefer forward repair.

Limits and follow-ups: members can still change expenses and participants directly through the API within their own trip (pre-existing, consistency enforced by commit-time checks); editing amounts of an old expense after a member left can create a balance for someone no longer in the trip (customer may want to restrict this); Excel/.ics downloads, Google OAuth and hosted email/Realtime were not exercised in the browser; production advisors list performance items (unindexed foreign keys, RLS init plans) that are not material at current size.
