# WanderPlan 0.10.0 — itinerary overlap warning (R11)

Status: released 18/10/2026 (Asia/Saigon). PR #4 merged as `d36d741`; feature commit `ab7a517`. Customer rules: A031; go-live approval A032.

## Scope

- Two activities on the same date overlap when their [start, end) ranges intersect; 09:00–10:00 and 10:00–11:00 do not.
- The add/edit form shows a live warning naming the overlapping activities and their times; saving is never blocked. An item being edited is not compared with itself; incomplete or inverted draft times do not warn (existing validation reports them).
- The itinerary list marks every overlapping activity with “Trùng giờ”, including rows from Excel import.
- Multi-day activities are deferred. No migration, RPC, Excel, demo storage or permission change; old data is unchanged.
