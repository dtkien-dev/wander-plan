# WanderPlan 0.9.2 — Frozen money for former members + mock UAT

## Changed (customer rule A024)

Once a member has left (after settling, see 0.9.1), every expense they paid for or took part in keeps its money exactly as recorded: estimate, actual, split, payer and participants cannot change and the expense cannot be deleted. Title, category, dates and notes stay editable. The form shows a read-only "Phần tiền đã khóa vì có người đã rời nhóm" summary, the delete button is disabled with an explanation, payments from someone who left can no longer be undone, and Excel rows that try to change such money are reported as errors. The database enforces the same rules for direct API writes; recording a new payment requires the payer to be a current member.
