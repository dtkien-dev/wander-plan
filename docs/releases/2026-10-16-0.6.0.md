# WanderPlan0.6.0 — Trip archive and restoration

Only trip owners can archive or restore a trip. Confirmation explains that itinerary, expenses, checklist and pending invitations become hidden until restored. Owners have a minimal archive list beside the trip selector, including when no active trips remain. Data and memberships are preserved.
